My ArtBox Story

How it started

Last year I had the opportunity to volunteer as an art teacher at Mother Margherita School in Kawangware. When I signed up, I thought I would simply be teaching children how to draw and paint. I didn't expect the experience to change the way I think about art.

Every week I met children who were full of ideas and excited to create. They didn't lack imagination. What they often lacked were the basic materials that make art possible. That stayed with me.

"What if every school had a box with everything needed to make art possible?"

That question became the beginning of ArtBox — art supplies, easy-to-follow activity ideas and creative projects that make it easier for children to explore, create and express themselves.

My hope is that this is just the beginning. If ArtBox can help more children discover the joy of creating, it will have been worth it.

— Jeremy Kieme, founder

Programme Overview

What is an ArtBox?

ArtBox is a complete resource kit that lets a school run an Art Club without sourcing a single material. Each box arrives packed and ready for immediate use — everything needed to run weekly art sessions for 50 students across one full school term. Alongside the supplies, every box carries ten weeks of art activities and a teacher facilitation guide, so any teacher can lead a session with confidence.
